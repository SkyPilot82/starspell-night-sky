How the sky is built. The final plate is the game's own dusk gradient (its exact color stops, so the horizon glow, the moon's seat and the meadow seam land where the engine expects them) carrying the painting's star field and texture — crisp stars from the zenith all the way down, dithered against banding. The live twinkle stars, hero sparks, moon, aurora and clouds still move on top; the blurry scaled dots are thinned to small accents. Dawn and versus keep their procedural skies, like the meadow plate does.
A bonus the backlight exposed: the meadow plate's 9/15 ridge cut had clipped the distant pink range into two flat-top "mesas" (hidden today behind the matching glow). The ridge mask was re-authored from the original painting's silhouette — the center range is whole again in the NEW home shot.

The sky today

Left: what the opening actually looks like (the live game, first frame). The meadow (right, the painted plate you like) is MJ art — but everything above it is procedural: a baked gradient plus ~230 soft-edged dot sprites scaled up as "stars". On a phone they read as faint blurry smudges on near-black — that is the complaint, and it's structural: the dot texture is a blurred disc, so no amount of tuning makes it crisp. The fix offered here: paint the whole sky column the way the meadow is painted, in the same flat-vector dusk style (every option below was fired with the meadow plate itself as the Midjourney style anchor, so the palettes match by construction).

Each sky card shows the transition test: the candidate stacked straight onto the live meadow plate. Every MJ frame grew a small dark silhouette ridge at its foot despite the prompt — that ridge is cut, per frame, just above its tallest peak (the first version of this page used one crude fixed crop, which left a black band at the seam on most frames — that band was the preview's bug, not the design's; it will NOT exist in the game). What remains is rose dusk meeting the meadow's own glow — the final cut blends the last few rows so the value step disappears entirely.

What a painted sky changes. Today's stars live on three parallax tiers and twinkle live; a painted plate is one plane. The likely build keeps a thin layer of live twinkle stars + the hero spark-stars on top of the painting, so the sky still breathes — the painting brings the depth and crispness, the engine brings the motion.
The moon, clouds and aurora are engine-drawn too. Options with a baked moon/aurora/clouds would replace those engine pieces (or we crop/avoid them). Options without stay compatible with everything that already moves. Neither is wrong — it's a verdict.
